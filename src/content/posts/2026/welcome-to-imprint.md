Imprint is designed for people who want their writing to take the foreground. It uses a calm visual system, a compact information architecture, and only a small amount of client-side JavaScript.

## A writing-first layout

The home page presents posts as a chronological stream. Each entry keeps the date, title, description, and tags visible without turning the page into a dashboard.

Article pages use a comfortable reading width and add a table of contents when a post contains more than one second- or third-level heading.

## Useful defaults

The theme includes:

- responsive layouts;
- light and dark color schemes;
- archives, categories, and tags;
- RSS and sitemap generation;
- canonical URLs and social metadata;
- reduced-motion support;
- GitHub Pages deployment.

Start by editing `src/config.ts`, then replace the example posts with your own Markdown files.
